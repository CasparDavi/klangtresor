Your Suno music. At home.

Suno makes the songs. KlangTresor keeps them, plays them, measures them and shows them, on every screen in your home.

Where it runs

One computer at home.

macOS, Windows, Linux or Docker. Set up with a double click (on Linux, one command). Your archive is a folder on its disk.

  • Needs Node.js and ffmpeg. The setup brings Node.js along; it fetches ffmpeg itself on Windows, on Mac and Linux one command does it.
  • Space: about 1.6 GB for the program and its models, plus 3.1 GB for Whisper; then about 160 MB per song with everything that belongs to it: WAV, instrument tracks, analysis, covers (measured on the author’s 343 songs).
  • The computer is the server. While its window is open, your vault is reachable; close it and the vault is off.

Who reads it

Every screen with a browser.

Laptop, iPhone, Android, iPad, tablet. Anything that runs a web browser in your home network. Nothing to install.

  • Scan a QR code on the computer and the phone opens your vault.
  • On the home screen like an app; on the iPhone without the address bar.
  • Home network only. There is no password: whoever is on your network and knows the address can listen. So do not open the vault to the outside on your router.

Collect

Suno fills it.
You keep it.

One click on the bookmark at Suno and on the red button, and the vault fetches what is new. If Suno changes, or your subscription ends, everything is already at home.

Morning run

Everything you made. In full.

Covers, moving covers, lyrics, playlists, comments and hearts. The audio (MP3 and lossless WAV) the vault takes straight from your downloads. Songs you have not yet transferred from Suno to your computer must, from September 2026, be released for download at Suno.

  • Private songs too, as long as they sit in one of your own playlists.
  • Your Suno login stays in your browser. A bookmark does the fetching; KlangTresor never sees a password.
  • Only new things travel. What is already at home is not fetched again.

Sound studio

A real studio.
In your browser.

Equaliser with bell filters, compressor, boombox. One chain, in the order an engineer would build it. Plus room calibration at your listening spot.

8-band equaliser

Eight bands. Your sound.

Start neutral, or let a song match the rest of your collection, its style group, its album or a reference song.

  • Every band shows its headroom. The song itself decides how much a band can be raised.
  • Matching to collection, style group, album or reference is computed from the measured spectra.
  • On the phone as eight sliders with presets.

Bell frame

Bells you can grab.

Six bell filters, free in frequency, height and width, plus two shelves.

  • Drag a bell to move it; the handles on its shoulders set its width.
  • Single or stacked: see each bell on its own, or how they add up.
  • A notch removes a single annoying frequency.

Compressor

Compressor. Or a gradation curve.

The classic knee with threshold, ratio and limiter, or a curve like in a photo editor: quiet point, loud point, gamma.

  • Two modes, one stage: compressor or gradation compressor, never both at once.
  • The curve is drawn live, with the gain reduction as it happens.
  • Gamma or sigmoid with a single slider, for those who think in pictures.

Boombox

One switch for everything.

Loudness, more bass, more treble, clear voice, night mode. Plus MEGA BASS, reverb, echo and stereo width.

  • Night mode turns down bass and treble, above all the lows that travel through walls.
  • Tempo faster or slower, with or without keeping the pitch.
  • A clip light warns when everything together gets too loud.

Calibration

Measured in your room.

The microphone of your computer measures your speakers where you sit. The spectrum shows the whole chain: what Suno delivered, and what your settings make of it.

  • Measured at the listening position, not in a lab.
  • Before and after side by side in one spectrum.

Sound Space

Your songs as a sky of stars, sorted by sound groups.

Songs that sound alike become neighbours. Size, brightness and corona tell plays, hearts and comments.

  • Closeness is sound, measured from the audio on your computer, in three dimensions.
  • Fly from song to song in a ship, a cube, a raven or a Martian saucer.

Analysis

Every song, measured.

The structure from Suno’s sections, the beat grid, and where the peaks hit full scale or something cancels out in mono.

  • The playhead runs through all lanes, so every finding has its place in the song.
  • Loudness targets for each platform: Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, club, broadcast.

Stays yours

A folder. Yours.

Back it up, move it, update the program. The archive stays.

Export

On a stick. For the car too.

Your vault to take along: a frozen archive for playing, without the WAV originals. Double-click its start script and it runs on another Mac or Windows PC. Plus a Music folder: MP3s named by title, cover in the file, for car radio, TV and VLC.

  • In stages: the program and your newest songs first, instrument tracks last.
  • Stop early and you still have something to show.

Good to know

  • KlangTresor does not make music. Songs are made on Suno; KlangTresor keeps and shows them.
  • It only fetches your own songs, with your own Suno login.
  • When Suno changes its website, the morning run may need an update. Everything already at home stays playable.
